# IoT, ICS, SCADA, and Embedded Systems

## IoT

Internet of Things devices connect sensors, controllers, appliances, and other physical objects to networks. Common weaknesses include default credentials, limited patching, insecure firmware, exposed management services, and long lifecycles.

## Embedded Systems

Embedded systems are specialized computing components built into larger devices. Security may be constrained by limited resources, proprietary software, safety requirements, and difficult upgrade processes.

## ICS and SCADA

Industrial Control Systems manage physical processes. SCADA systems provide supervisory monitoring and control across distributed environments. Availability, safety, deterministic operation, and process integrity can be more important than rapid software changes.

## Security Architecture

Use network segmentation, strict management access, asset inventories, secure remote access, allowlisting where appropriate, monitoring, vendor-supported updates, and compensating controls for systems that cannot be patched.

## Safety Considerations

Security changes in operational technology should be tested carefully because an incorrect change can affect physical processes and safety. Maintenance windows and documented change procedures are essential.

## Exam Focus

Traditional IT can often prioritize confidentiality and rapid patching; OT environments frequently require careful balancing of availability, integrity, safety, and change risk.
