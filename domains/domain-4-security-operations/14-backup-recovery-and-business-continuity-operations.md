# Backup, Recovery, and Business Continuity Operations

## Backup Operations

Backups should be scheduled according to business recovery requirements, protected from unauthorized modification, monitored for failures, and periodically restored to verify usability.

## Recovery

Recovery operations prioritize critical services according to business impact and defined recovery objectives. Dependencies such as identity, DNS, networking, databases, and third-party services must be considered.

## RTO and RPO

RTO defines how quickly a service must be restored. RPO defines how much recent data the organization can afford to lose.

## Resilience

Use redundancy, alternate infrastructure, geographic separation, tested backups, and documented recovery procedures to reduce the impact of outages and destructive incidents.

## Exam Focus

A backup that cannot be restored is not a reliable recovery capability. Recovery testing is therefore an operational security activity, not merely a disaster-recovery planning exercise.
