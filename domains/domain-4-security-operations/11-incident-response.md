# Incident Response

## Incident

A security incident is an event or series of events that threatens confidentiality, integrity, availability, or policy compliance and requires response.

## Incident Response Lifecycle

A practical lifecycle includes preparation, detection and analysis, containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident activity. Organizations may use different terminology, but the core objective is controlled handling from initial detection through lessons learned.

## Preparation

Prepare policies, roles, communication channels, tools, backups, contact lists, playbooks, logging, training, and legal requirements before incidents occur.

## Detection and Analysis

Validate alerts, identify affected assets, establish a timeline, determine scope, preserve relevant evidence, and assess business impact.

## Containment

Short-term containment limits immediate spread or damage. Long-term containment provides a more sustainable controlled state while eradication is prepared.

## Eradication

Remove malicious artifacts, close exploited vulnerabilities, reset compromised credentials, eliminate persistence, and address the root cause.

## Recovery

Restore systems safely, validate security controls, monitor for recurrence, and return operations to normal.

## Post-Incident Activity

Document the incident, conduct lessons learned, improve controls and playbooks, update detections, and track corrective actions.

## Exam Focus

Containment limits damage, eradication removes the cause or malicious presence, and recovery restores normal operations. Do not confuse containment with eradication.
