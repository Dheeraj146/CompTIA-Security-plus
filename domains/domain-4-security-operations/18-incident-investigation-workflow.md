# Incident Investigation Workflow

## 1. Validate

Determine whether the event represents suspicious activity, a policy violation, or a confirmed incident.

## 2. Scope

Identify affected accounts, hosts, applications, networks, data, and time periods.

## 3. Timeline

Correlate timestamps from authentication, endpoint, network, application, and cloud sources. Account for clock differences and time zones.

## 4. Preserve

Protect relevant evidence before remediation actions alter or destroy it. Follow organizational and legal requirements.

## 5. Analyze

Determine initial access, affected resources, persistence, lateral movement, data access, and other relevant activity while avoiding unsupported conclusions.

## 6. Respond

Contain the incident, eradicate malicious presence, recover systems, and increase monitoring.

## 7. Document

Record decisions, evidence, actions, responsible personnel, communications, and final conclusions. Documentation should support lessons learned and future detection improvements.

## Exam Focus

Investigation should be evidence-driven and documented. Containment may need to balance security, evidence preservation, and business continuity.
