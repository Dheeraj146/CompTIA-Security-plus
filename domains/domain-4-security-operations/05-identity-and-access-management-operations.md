# Identity and Access Management Operations

## Identity Lifecycle

IAM operations cover account creation, identity verification, role assignment, authentication, authorization, periodic review, modification, and timely removal of access.

## Joiner, Mover, Leaver

New users should receive only required access. When responsibilities change, permissions should be adjusted to match the new role. When a person leaves, accounts, sessions, tokens, keys, and physical access should be revoked promptly.

## Access Reviews

Periodic reviews identify unnecessary privileges, dormant accounts, excessive permissions, orphaned accounts, and segregation-of-duties conflicts.

## Privileged Access Management

PAM controls highly privileged identities through vaulting, approval, just-in-time access, session monitoring, credential rotation, and auditing.

## Authentication Operations

Authentication verifies identity; authorization determines permitted actions; accounting records activity. MFA adds independent authentication factors and reduces the impact of password compromise.

## Exam Focus

Least privilege means minimum required access. Need-to-know limits access to information. Account lifecycle management prevents lingering access after role changes or termination.
